## Raw content

### Regulatory framework

- The Financial Services Authority (**OJK**) assumed regulatory oversight of
  digital assets on **10 January 2025**, replacing **Bappebti**.
- In **March 2026**, OJK "released an official whitelist of licensed and
  registered digital financial asset trading operators."

### Licensed exchanges profiled

**1. Indodax**
- Co-founded by Oscar Darmawan and William Sutanto.
- CEO: William Sutanto (from May 2025).
- Strategic partnership with HashKey Exchange for liquidity and Real-World-Asset
  development.

**2. Tokocrypto**
- Binance-backed exchange.
- Transaction volume: US$4 billion (2023) → US$12 billion (2024).
- CEO: Calvin Kizana.
- Achieved first annual profit in 2024.

**3. Pintu**
- Mobile-first platform targeting retail investors.
- 11+ million app downloads; ~400 listed tokens.
- Series B: US$113 million (Lightspeed, Pantera Capital).
- Pintu Academy for user education.

**4. Reku** (formerly Rekeningku)
- "Over 800 cryptocurrencies" plus US stocks and ETFs.
- Portfolio-tracking features.

**5. Pluang**
- All-in-one investment app.
- 13+ million registered accounts; group revenue US$30 million.
- Series C: US$10 million (MUFG Innovation Partners).
- Profitable in 2025.

**6. FLOQ** (formerly PT Kripto Maksima Koin, GoTo Group)
- CEO: Yudhono Rawis (former Tokocrypto CEO).
- 1.8 million registered users.
- Funding: US$11.3 million (June 2026) from Ascent HFX Group and MD Capital.
- Target: 3 million users by end of 2026.
