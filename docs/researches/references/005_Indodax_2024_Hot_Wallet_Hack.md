## Raw content

- On **10 September 2024**, Indodax (Indonesia's oldest and largest homegrown
  crypto exchange) suffered a security breach. Loss estimates range from
  **~US$22 million** (Yahoo/CoinDesk headline) to **~US$25.47 million** across
  multiple assets — reported components include ~25 BTC (~US$1.4M), ~US$14M in
  ETH, ~US$2.4M in TRX, ~US$2.5M in MATIC.
- Indodax **temporarily paused operations** ("maintenance" / security issue),
  halted withdrawals from the compromised hot wallet, and moved remaining funds
  to more secure storage — limiting further loss.
- Indodax **publicly pledged to reimburse** all affected users and warned about
  follow-on scams impersonating the exchange.
- Blockchain security firm **SlowMist** suggested the **withdrawal system** was
  compromised rather than a straightforwardly leaked hot-wallet key. Attribution
  reporting linked the attack pattern to the **Lazarus Group** (North Korea).
- Exchange resumed services in stages over the following weeks; user balances
  were reported made whole.
