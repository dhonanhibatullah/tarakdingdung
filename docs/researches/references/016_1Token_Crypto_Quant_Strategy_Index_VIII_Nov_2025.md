## Raw content

### Index methodology

- **Contributors:** 11 crypto trading teams total (with overlap): 9 in Funding
  Arbitrage, 4 in Long-Short.
- **Total AUM:** over $4 billion.
- **Calculation:** Time-Weighted Return (TWR), arithmetic-mean aggregation.
- **Period:** 2025-11-01 to 2025-12-01.
- **Exchanges:** Binance, OKX, Bybit, Gate, LTP Rapidx.
- **Risk-free rate:** 4.5%.

### Long-Short strategy

- Reports Sharpe, Sortino, Calmar; specific numeric monthly return not disclosed
  in the extracted content.
- "Long-short strategies exhibit superior liquidity" with shorter turnover
  cycles.
- Coin concentration β = 0.073 → "highly diversified," slight large-cap tilt.

### Funding Arbitrage strategy

- Sharpe "excluded from analysis due to relative strategy stability" (i.e. return
  stream is smooth enough that Sharpe is uninformative / very high).
- PnL decomposed into: funding income, trading fees, interest paid, trading P&L.
- New metric: **Funding Yield per Gross Exposure (FYpGE)** — measures funding
  income efficiency per unit of gross exposure.
- Coin concentration β = 0.135 → "highly diversified," heavier BTC/ETH weight.

### Notable observations

- Both strategies highly diversified across coin holdings in Nov 2025.
- Delta-neutral funding arbitrage is treated as the "stable income" leg; directional
  long-short carries the risk/return.
