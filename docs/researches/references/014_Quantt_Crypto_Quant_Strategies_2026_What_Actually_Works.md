## Raw content

### Strategies identified as viable in 2026

**1. Market making**
- Venues: spot (Binance, Coinbase), perpetual swaps, options (Deribit).
- Capital: "$5M–$50M+ capital and serious infrastructure investment."
- Reality: "Spreads have compressed significantly. BTC-USDT spreads on Binance
  are 1-2 bps; ETH-USDT 1-3 bps" (vs 50–100 bps in 2017). Smaller operators are
  "at a structural disadvantage."

**2. Basis trading (cash-and-carry)**
- Returns: "8-15% annualised in normal conditions" (down from "30-50% annualised
  yields" in 2020–2021).
- Funding yield range: "-10% to +30% depending on conditions."

**3. Statistical arbitrage**
- Works: "cross-sectional momentum/reversion strategies"; time-of-day patterns
  during the "US/Asia overlap."
- Doesn't work: "naive cointegration on most cross-asset pairs" due to frequent
  regime shifts.

**4. MEV extraction**
- Capital: "typically require $10M+ capital and a small team."
- Ethereum MEV "highly competitive"; Solana MEV "growing"; L2 "less mature."

**5. Volatility trading**
- Deribit options; "crypto vol surfaces are less efficient than equity vol
  surfaces."

**6. On-chain analytics**
- Effective at "1-7 day horizons"; "long-horizon predictions from on-chain data
  alone" do not work.

**7. Funding rate arbitrage**
- Variants: cross-exchange strategies and funding-rate prediction.

**8. Trend following**
- "Higher volatility means whipsaw risk is severe"; needs "strict risk
  management" and a "multi-timeframe approach."

### What doesn't work

- Strategies from 2020–2021 ("heavy retail presence is gone").
- Whale-following strategies (survivorship bias).
- Solo MEV operations (now needs teams + infrastructure).

### Infrastructure cited

- Self-hosted Ethereum node (or Solana validator); private mempool access.
- Market data: Kaiko, Tardis, Amberdata.
- On-chain: Dune Analytics, Nansen, Glassnode.

### Operational risks

- "Exchange counterparty risk — if Binance fails, your hedge doesn't help."
- "Avoid keeping more than 30% of total capital on any single exchange."

### Benchmark figures (from search-result excerpt of this page / sibling pages)

- Dollar-neutral strategies "returned 31.23% as an industry benchmark in 2025,
  with the top-performing venue hitting 66.69% with a Sharpe ratio of 2.39."
- "Cash-and-carry basis trades have historically hit a Sharpe of 4.84, while
  statistical arbitrage on BTC-ETH clocks in at 2.23."
