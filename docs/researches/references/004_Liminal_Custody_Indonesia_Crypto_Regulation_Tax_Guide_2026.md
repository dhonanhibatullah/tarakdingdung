## Raw content

### Regulatory transition

- Crypto supervision transitioned from **Bappebti** (commodity regulator) to
  **OJK** (financial-services authority).
- A **National Crypto Exchange (CFX)** now acts as the central venue for
  licensed trading and clearing.
- Licensed exchanges operate as **Digital Financial Asset Traders (PAKD)** under
  OJK oversight and route local trading through the CFX structure.

### Tax treatment

- Local exchanges licensed as PAKD and connected to CFX "apply automated tax
  handling on eligible trades," with **0.21% final income tax (PPh)** withheld
  and remitted.
- Traders using offshore platforms without a compliant local bridge may face
  "a higher 1% tax bracket on certain self-reported transactions" without the
  automatic-withholding benefit.

### Licensed Indonesian exchanges named

- **Indodax** (est. 2014) — OJK-regulated PAKD; deep IDR liquidity; local
  payment options.
- **Tokocrypto** (est. 2018) — OJK-regulated PAKD; 100% Binance-owned; CFX
  integration.
- **Pintu** (est. 2020) — OJK-approved PAKD; mobile-first; Pintu Pro and Futures
  products.
- **Reku / Rekeningku** (est. 2017) — Bappebti-registered; transparent fees;
  24/7 local support.
- **Upbit Indonesia** (late 2010s) — Bappebti-registered; Korean-backed
  infrastructure.
